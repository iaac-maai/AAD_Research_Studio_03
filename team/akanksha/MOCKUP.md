# Fab-lab column mock-up

## Purpose

Build one small, visible column cage that Diego can photograph and use as a held-out test set. The mock-up must show normal construction geometry and three isolated defects.

## Build dimensions

Use a 400 x 400 x 1200 mm cage mock-up.

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Longitudinal bars | 4 bars, 16 mm diameter, vertical |
| Corners | Bars at the four 400 mm cage corners |
| Ties | 8 mm tie diameter |
| Cover to tie centreline target | 30 mm, outside bar/tie clearance for the mock-up |
| End-zone tie spacing | 100 mm in the first 500 mm at each end, or the lab-safe nearest repeatable spacing |
| Middle-zone tie spacing | 200 mm |
| Hook angle | 135 degrees on all correctly made ties |
| Hook tail | 80 mm minimum on correctly made ties |
| Base plate | Flat, stable board approximately 500 x 500 mm |

The 100 mm end spacing is deliberately tighter than the 128 mm DCM code limit so one of the schedule values is not an end-zone code question.

## Materials and mock build

- 16 mm inert rods or commercially available mock rebar.
- 8 mm wire for ties.
- A rigid 500 x 500 mm plywood or foam board base.
- Cable ties or removable clips for temporary positioning.
- Markers 0–3 printed by `python pipeline.py markers`; place four markers outside the cage corners and never on the rebar.
- A 1 m ruler and 100 mm square for final measurements.

Do not pour concrete, cut structural reinforcement for this mock-up, or leave sharp exposed ends within the lab work area.

## Planned ties

At the first 500 mm from both ends:

`0, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500 mm`

Repeat every 200 mm in the middle.

End zones and middle ties must be visibly labelled on Diego's measurement sheet, but labels should not appear as rebar in every photo.

## Planted faults

Use one isolated fault at a time in the final test set:

1. **Missing tie:** omit one tie from the lower end zone.
2. **Wrong hook:** make one tie hook 90 degrees instead of 135 degrees.
3. **Shifted bar:** move one longitudinal bar approximately 30 mm laterally from its scheduled corner position. It must not make the cage unstable.

Keep a corrected version of the cage, and a second face for each fault. Photograph both corrected and faulted states. For photography, record the face A/B/C/D and top/end position.

## Photography schedule

The Saturday build is complete only when every face can be photographed from a stable position:

1. Sunday 14:00–14:30: print and verify markers.
2. 14:30–14:45: build and secure the corrected cage.
3. 14:45–15:15: photograph corrected cage, all four faces.
4. 15:15–16:00: introduce faults one at a time and photograph each state.
5. 16:00–16:30: restore the cage and photograph one final corrected state.

## Check before lab

- Measure a marker and confirm it is 100 mm after printing.
- Confirm all tie and bar diameters with a calliper.
- Confirm one 135-degree hook and one 80 mm tail with the lab tools.
- Confirm the cage is stable and cannot fall during photography.
