# ROI-1555 in our Roboflow project

Diego, Fri 27 Nov 2026.

**What it is:** 1,555 real photos with polygon outlines, two classes: `straight` (bars) and `hoop` (stirrups).
Sun, Fan & Shao (2025), *Deep learning-based rebar detection and instance segmentation in images*,
Advanced Engineering Informatics 65, 103224.
Hugging Face: `tsrobcvai/ROI-1555_Rebar_Detection_and_Instance_Segmentation_Dataset`.

**Why it matters:** it is the only real-photo source with stirrup outlines. Our baseline found 5 stirrups
on 60 real photos; ROI-1555 adds 2,677 real stirrups.

## What went in

| Split | Photos |
|---|---|
| train (1260 set, 90 %) | 1,134 |
| valid (1260 set, 10 %) | 126 |
| test (paper scenes scen1-3) | 295 |

Objects after merging pieces: **7,785 bars and 2,677 stirrups** (the raw files hold 22,355 and 8,987 pieces).

**Pieces vs objects (fixed 28 Nov):** ROI-1555 draws every *visible piece* as its own polygon, and gives the
pieces of one object the same label (`hoop-3` twice = one stirrup seen in two pieces). The first upload kept
every piece as a separate instance, so one cage photo counted 26 stirrups and 133 bars instead of 5 and 24.
The converter now merges pieces with the same label into one instance, joined by thin bridges across the
hidden parts (the same rule as our synthetic labels), and the corrected labels replaced the old ones in Roboflow.

- Converted with `roi1555_to_yolo.py`: `straight -> bar`, `hoop -> stirrup`, pieces of one object merged. The hoop
  outlines follow the steel and leave the inside of the loop empty, the same as our rule.
- Uploaded to Roboflow: batch `roi1555`, tags `roi1555` + `no_licence_research_only`. 1,549 stored, 6 duplicates
  inside the set.
- 183 photos with clearly visible stirrup ends are tagged `hooks_todo` for hand-labelled hooks (paused for now:
  the team first tests measuring hook angles from the stirrup outline).
- Most photos are loose bars, mesh and stirrups on a floor or a lab rig, not column cages on site.
- **Hooks:** many stirrups end in visible 135° hooks, but they are inside the `hoop` outline with no label of their
  own. Next step: draw `hook_90` / `hook_135` on top (rule in LABELLING.md).
