# ROI-1555 in our Roboflow project

Diego, Fri 27 Nov 2026.

**What it is:** 1,555 real photos with polygon outlines, two classes: `straight` (bars) and `hoop` (stirrups).
Sun, Fan & Shao (2025), *Deep learning-based rebar detection and instance segmentation in images*,
Advanced Engineering Informatics 65, 103224.
Hugging Face: `tsrobcvai/ROI-1555_Rebar_Detection_and_Instance_Segmentation_Dataset`.

**Why it matters:** it is the only real-photo source with stirrup outlines. Our baseline found 5 stirrups
on 60 real photos; ROI-1555 adds 8,987 real stirrup outlines.

## What went in

| Split | Photos | bar | stirrup |
|---|---|---|---|
| train (1260 set, 90 %) | 1,134 | 16,533 | 7,294 |
| valid (1260 set, 10 %) | 126 | 2,125 | 996 |
| test (paper scenes scen1-3) | 295 | 3,697 | 697 |

- Converted with `roi1555_to_yolo.py`: `straight -> bar`, `hoop -> stirrup`. The hoop outlines follow the steel
  and leave the inside of the loop empty, the same as our rule.
- Uploaded to Roboflow: batch `roi1555`, tags `roi1555` + `no_licence_research_only`. 1,549 stored, 6 duplicates
  inside the set. A few degenerate polygons (zero area) were dropped by Roboflow.
- Most photos are loose bars, mesh and stirrups on a floor or a lab rig, not column cages on site.
- **Hooks:** many stirrups end in visible 135° hooks, but they are inside the `hoop` outline with no label of their
  own. Next step: draw `hook_90` / `hook_135` on top (rule in LABELLING.md).
